Facials Curated for Skin and Soul

The SkinSoul Collective pairs results-driven facials and LED light therapy with an unhurried, restorative ritual. Every treatment is tailored to your skin's needs on the day you arrive.

Expect visible glow, calmer skin, and a reset for your mind as much as your complexion.

  • LED light therapy
  • Hydration and glow boosts
  • Support for acne and congestion
  • Calming, sensitive-skin care
  • Professional-grade products
  • Home-care recommendations
  • A deeply relaxing ritual

What to Expect

Your esthetic ritual begins with a skin analysis, followed by a customized facial and LED therapy. You will leave with a simple home routine to keep your results going.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a facial?

Every four to six weeks keeps skin balanced and glowing, though your plan may vary.

Is LED light therapy safe?

Yes. LED therapy is gentle, non-invasive, and suitable for most skin types.

Can I wear makeup afterward?

We recommend letting your skin breathe for the rest of the day for the best results.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Every facial is customized, and sensitive skin receives gentle, calming products and techniques.
