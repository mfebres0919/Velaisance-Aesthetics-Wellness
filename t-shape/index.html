The Treatment

Three Technologies, One Session

T-Shape 2 by Baldan combines radiofrequency, vacuum massage, and low-level laser technology in one non-invasive session. It is FDA-cleared to temporarily reduce the appearance of cellulite, temporarily improve local circulation, and relieve minor muscle aches and spasms.
